# Code of Conduct

Boring exists to collect the lessons software engineers keep relearning. That works
only if the people contributing and asking questions are treated well.

## Our standard

- **Be precise and kind.** Correct each other's facts, not each other's worth. Sources
  beat opinions here — bring one when you disagree.
- **Assume competence.** Everyone forgot something once. That is the entire reason this
  repository exists.
- **Respect expertise boundaries.** Security, accessibility, and internationalization
  have deep specialist communities; cite their work rather than talking over it.
- **No harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing of others'
  private information.** Sexualized language and unwelcome attention of any kind are
  out of bounds everywhere the community meets (issues, PRs, chats, conferences).

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
chat channels, and events — and to anyone representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the maintainers at **conduct@boring.example** (replace with the
real address before first public release). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behavior violates this code. Decisions and their reasons are
communicated to the reporter. Escalation and appeal paths will be documented as the
maintainer team grows.
